1. Native template bindings (template-quality-metadata fixture):
   prod/sig/sig2/sigT/sigT2 share one q0 between quality-sensitive arguments
   and conclusion. Exact fields are now part of fresh NativeMeta checks.
   Eight records pass and five metadata mutations fail.

2. Two representation families (template-quality-branches fixture):
   all-Prop product/dependent sum can be Prop inductives.
   Type family permits Sort fields, with a non-Prop result floor.
   Eight source and target combinations and beta laws pass, no target axioms.
   This prototype is not connected to translator dispatch.

3. Annotation versus conversion (template-quality-conversion fixture):
   direct prod True True and sigT True (fun _ => True) are Prop.
   A transparent wrapper with declared Type result cannot itself be assigned
   a Prop annotation; Rocq rejects those two attempted declarations.
   Nevertheless each wrapper application is definitionally equal (eq_refl)
   to its delta/beta-expanded direct expression. Therefore do NOT infer that
   there is a permanently fixed explicit quality argument at every occurrence.
   Preserve the declaration's advertised result sort and implement any required
   cumulative transport while permitting checked source conversion.

   All four template references have EMPTY explicit quality and level instances.
   Re-typing the full applications in their SOURCE binder environments gives:
     direct_prod_pp: Prop
     through_type_prod body under A:Type,B:Type: Type
     direct_sigma_pp: Prop
     through_type_sigma body under A:Type,B:A->Type: Type
   The diagnostic visits lambda/product/let contexts, and deliberately skips
   cases/fixpoints. It is optional diagnostic output, not acceptance metadata,
   not a general retyper, and not a new success rule.

Next implementation requirement:
   independently checked source-context quality determination, coherent target
   family dispatch for inductive/constructor/case/projection references, and
   explicit correspondence through source cumulative conversions.
   Do not guess quality solely from target argument sorts after generalization,

   All source-admissible branches must be represented; a Type-only acceptance
   is not complete coverage of a quality-polymorphic declaration.
